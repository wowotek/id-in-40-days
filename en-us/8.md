# Day 8 – Everyday Verbs 🏃‍♀️📚

Verbs are the heart of any sentence. Today you'll learn the most **commonly used Indonesian verbs** for daily life — no conjugation needed!

Indonesian verbs are **simple** and do **not change** based on subject or tense. You only need to remember the root word (kata dasar) and add context (like time markers).

## 🧠 Verb Facts

- Indonesian verbs do **not** change for tense:  
  → "Saya makan" can mean *I eat*, *I am eating*, or *I ate*, depending on time context.

- Add time markers for clarity:
  - **sudah** = already
  - **sedang** = currently
  - **akan** = will

> Example:  
> Saya **sudah makan**. → I already ate.  
> Saya **akan tidur**. → I will sleep.
