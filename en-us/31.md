# Day 31 — Talking About Work and School

Today you'll learn how to discuss your job, studies, and daily responsibilities in Indonesian. This is useful for introductions and conversations!

---

## 🏫 Work & School Vocabulary

| Indonesian         | English           |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| pekerjaan          | job/work          |
| kantor             | office            |
| guru               | teacher           |
| murid              | student           |
| sekolah            | school            |
| universitas        | university        |
| belajar            | to study          |
| mengajar           | to teach          |
| rapat              | meeting           |
| tugas              | assignment/task   |
| libur              | holiday           |

---

## 🗣️ Talking About Work and School

| Indonesian                              | English                          |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Saya bekerja di kantor.                 | I work at an office.             |
| Dia seorang guru di sekolah dasar.      | He/She is a teacher at an elementary school. |
| Kami belajar di universitas.            | We study at university.          |
| Ada rapat jam sepuluh.                  | There is a meeting at ten.       |
| Saya punya banyak tugas hari ini.       | I have a lot of assignments today.|

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**Talking about work and school:**

> **Anda:** Apa pekerjaanmu?
> 
> **Teman:** Saya guru bahasa Inggris.
> 
> **Anda:** Di mana kamu mengajar?
> 
> **Teman:** Di sekolah menengah pertama.

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. I am a student.
2. She works at a hospital.
3. We have a meeting today.
4. He studies at university.
5. I have a lot of homework.

✅ _Answer Key:_

1. Saya murid.
2. Dia bekerja di rumah sakit.
3. Kami ada rapat hari ini.
4. Dia belajar di universitas.
5. Saya punya banyak tugas.
