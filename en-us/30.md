# Day 30 — Hobbies and Free Time

Today you'll learn how to talk about your hobbies and what you do in your free time in Indonesian. Share your interests with others!

---

## 🎨 Hobbies Vocabulary

| Indonesian         | English           |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| hobi               | hobby             |
| membaca            | reading           |
| menulis            | writing           |
| menonton           | watching          |
| bermain            | playing           |
| olahraga           | sports/exercise   |
| memasak            | cooking           |
| berjalan-jalan     | walking/traveling |
| mendengarkan musik | listening to music|
| berkebun           | gardening         |
| memancing          | fishing           |

---

## 🗣️ Talking About Hobbies

| Indonesian                              | English                          |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Hobi saya membaca buku.                 | My hobby is reading books.       |
| Dia suka memasak di rumah.              | He/She likes cooking at home.    |
| Kami sering bermain bola.               | We often play football.          |
| Apa hobi favoritmu?                     | What is your favorite hobby?     |
| Saya suka mendengarkan musik.           | I like listening to music.       |

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**Talking about free time:**

> **Anda:** Apa yang kamu lakukan di waktu luang?
> 
> **Teman:** Saya suka berkebun dan membaca.
> 
> **Anda:** Saya lebih suka menonton film.

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. My hobby is cooking.
2. He likes playing guitar.
3. We often go walking.
4. What is your favorite hobby?
5. I like fishing.

✅ _Answer Key:_

1. Hobi saya memasak.
2. Dia suka bermain gitar.
3. Kami sering berjalan-jalan.
4. Hobi favoritmu apa?
5. Saya suka memancing.
