# Day 20 — Future Plans

Today you'll learn how to talk about the future in Indonesian. Expressing future intentions is simple and direct!

---

## 🔮 Future Tense Vocabulary & Markers

| Indonesian     | English         |
|----------------|----------------|
| akan           | will           |
| nanti          | later          |
| besok          | tomorrow       |
| minggu depan   | next week      |
| tahun depan    | next year      |
| sebentar lagi  | soon           |
| rencana        | plan           |

---

## 🗣️ Talking About the Future

| Indonesian                        | English                        |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Saya akan pergi.                  | I will go.                     |
| Kamu akan belajar besok?          | Will you study tomorrow?       |
| Kami akan bertemu minggu depan.   | We will meet next week.        |
| Nanti saya telepon kamu.          | I'll call you later.           |
| Tahun depan dia ke Indonesia.     | Next year he/she goes to Indonesia. |

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**Making plans with a friend:**

> **Anda:** Besok kamu ada rencana?
> 
> **Teman:** Saya akan belajar di rumah.
> 
> **Anda:** Minggu depan kita jalan-jalan?
> 
> **Teman:** Boleh, saya suka!

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. I will go to the market tomorrow.
2. We will meet next week.
3. She will call you later.
4. Do you have plans for next year?
5. I will study soon.

✅ _Answer Key:_

1. Saya akan ke pasar besok.
2. Kami akan bertemu minggu depan.
3. Dia akan telepon kamu nanti.
4. Kamu ada rencana tahun depan?
5. Saya akan belajar sebentar lagi.
