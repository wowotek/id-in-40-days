# 🌳 Day 15 — Nature & Weather

Today you'll learn how to talk about the natural world and the weather in Indonesian. Perfect for chatting about your surroundings or making small talk!

## ☀️ Talking About the Weather

| Indonesian                        | English                        |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Cuacanya panas hari ini.          | It's hot today.                |
| Hari ini hujan.                   | It's raining today.            |
| Besok cerah.                      | Tomorrow will be sunny.        |
| Anginnya kencang.                 | The wind is strong.            |
| Udara sangat lembab.              | The air is very humid.         |
| Saya suka musim hujan.            | I like the rainy season.       |

---

## 🌱 Example Dialogue

**At the park:**

> **Anda:** Cuacanya bagus hari ini, ya?
> 
> **Teman:** Iya, cerah dan tidak terlalu panas.
> 
> **Anda:** Saya suka kalau berangin sedikit.
> 
> **Teman:** Aku juga! Mau jalan-jalan?

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. It's raining today.
2. The weather is hot.
