# Day 39 — Telling Stories and Experiences

Today you'll learn how to narrate past events, share stories, and talk about experiences in Indonesian. This is great for connecting with others!

---

## 📖 Storytelling Vocabulary

| Indonesian         | English           |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| cerita             | story             |
| pengalaman         | experience        |
| dulu               | in the past       |
| waktu itu          | at that time      |
| pernah             | ever/once         |
| belum pernah       | never             |
| kejadian           | event             |
| liburan            | holiday/vacation  |
| petualangan        | adventure         |
| mendengar          | to hear           |
| melihat            | to see            |

---

## 🗣️ Telling Stories and Experiences

| Indonesian                              | English                          |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Saya pernah ke Bali.                    | I have been to Bali.             |
| Dulu saya tinggal di Bandung.           | I used to live in Bandung.       |
| Waktu itu, kami bertemu di pasar.       | At that time, we met at the market.|
| Ceritakan pengalamanmu!                 | Tell your experience!            |
| Saya belum pernah mencoba itu.          | I have never tried that.         |

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**Sharing an experience:**

> **Anda:** Pernah naik gunung?
> 
> **Teman:** Pernah, waktu SMA. Itu petualangan seru!
> 
> **Anda:** Ceritakan dong!

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. I have never been to Bali.
2. Tell your story!
3. We met at the station.
4. He has tried it before.
5. That was an interesting experience.

✅ _Answer Key:_

1. Saya belum pernah ke Bali.
2. Ceritakan ceritamu!
3. Kami bertemu di stasiun.
4. Dia pernah mencoba itu.
5. Itu pengalaman menarik.
