# ⏳ Day 19 — Past Tense Structures

Today you'll learn how to talk about the past in Indonesian. Indonesian uses simple markers to indicate past actions—no complicated verb changes!

---

## 🕰️ Past Tense Vocabulary & Markers

| Indonesian     | English         |
|----------------|----------------|
| sudah          | already        |
| belum          | not yet        |
| tadi           | earlier        |
| kemarin        | yesterday      |
| minggu lalu    | last week      |
| tahun lalu     | last year      |
| baru saja      | just now       |
| dulu           | in the past    |

---

## 🗣️ Talking About the Past

| Indonesian                        | English                        |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Saya sudah makan.                 | I have eaten.                  |
| Kamu sudah pergi?                 | Have you gone?                 |
| Saya belum selesai.               | I'm not finished yet.          |
| Tadi saya ke pasar.               | I went to the market earlier.  |
| Kemarin dia sakit.                | He/She was sick yesterday.     |
| Minggu lalu kami liburan.         | We were on holiday last week.  |

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**Talking with a friend:**

> **Anda:** Sudah makan?
> 
> **Teman:** Sudah, tadi saya makan nasi goreng.
> 
> **Anda:** Kemarin kamu ke mana?
> 
> **Teman:** Saya di rumah saja.

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. I have already finished.

4. Have you eaten?
5. I was at home just now.

1. Saya sudah selesai.
2. Dia sakit kemarin.
3. Kami ke pasar minggu lalu.
