# Day 36 — Feelings and Emotions

Today you'll learn how to express your feelings and emotions in Indonesian. This is important for sharing your mood and understanding others!

---

## 😊 Feelings & Emotions Vocabulary

| Indonesian         | English           |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| senang             | happy             |
| sedih              | sad               |
| marah              | angry             |
| takut              | afraid            |
| cemas              | anxious           |
| bangga             | proud             |
| kecewa             | disappointed      |
| terkejut           | surprised         |
| tenang             | calm              |
| lelah              | tired             |
| semangat           | enthusiastic      |

---

## 🗣️ Expressing Feelings

| Indonesian                              | English                          |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Saya merasa senang hari ini.            | I feel happy today.              |
| Dia sedih karena gagal ujian.           | He/She is sad because of failing the exam. |
| Kami bangga dengan hasilnya.            | We are proud of the result.      |
| Jangan marah, ya!                       | Don't be angry, okay!            |
| Saya lelah setelah bekerja.             | I am tired after working.        |

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**Expressing emotions:**

> **Anda:** Kenapa kamu terlihat cemas?
> 
> **Teman:** Saya takut ujian besok.
> 
> **Anda:** Semangat! Kamu pasti bisa.

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. I am happy.

5. Are you sad?
