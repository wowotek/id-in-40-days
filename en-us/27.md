# Day 27 — Transportation and Directions

Today you'll learn how to get around, use transportation, and ask for directions in Indonesian. These skills are essential for travel!

---

## 🚗 Transportation Vocabulary

| Indonesian         | English           |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| mobil              | car               |
| motor              | motorcycle        |
| sepeda             | bicycle           |
| bus                | bus               |
| kereta             | train             |
| pesawat            | airplane          |
| jalan              | street/road       |
| belok              | turn              |
| lurus              | straight          |
| kiri               | left              |
| kanan              | right             |
| halte              | bus stop          |
| stasiun            | station           |

---

## 🗣️ Asking for Directions

| Indonesian                              | English                          |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Di mana halte bus?                      | Where is the bus stop?           |
| Bagaimana ke stasiun?                   | How do I get to the station?     |
| Belok kiri di lampu merah.              | Turn left at the traffic light.  |
| Jalan lurus, lalu belok kanan.          | Go straight, then turn right.    |
| Apakah ini jalan ke bandara?            | Is this the way to the airport?  |

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**Asking for directions:**

> **Anda:** Permisi, di mana stasiun kereta?
> 
> **Orang:** Jalan lurus, lalu belok kanan di perempatan.
> 
> **Anda:** Terima kasih!
> 
> **Orang:** Sama-sama.

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. Where is the bus stop?
2. How do I get to the airport?
3. Turn left at the corner.
4. Go straight, then turn right.
5. Is this the way to the market?

✅ _Answer Key:_

1. Di mana halte bus?
2. Bagaimana ke bandara?
3. Belok kiri di pojok.
4. Jalan lurus, lalu belok kanan.
5. Apakah ini jalan ke pasar?
