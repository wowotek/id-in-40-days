# Day 2 – Basic Greetings & Introductions
One of the first steps to mastering any language is being able to greet people and introduce yourself. In Indonesian, greetings are simple, friendly, and flexible so you can use them every day.

## Common Greetings
| Indonesian          | Meaning                    | Notes                       |
| ------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Halo**            | Hello                      | Universal and casual.       |
| **Selamat pagi**    | Good morning               | Use until about 11 AM.      |
| **Selamat siang**   | Good day / early afternoon | From around 11 AM to 3 PM.  |
| **Selamat sore**    | Good afternoon / evening   | From around 3 PM to sunset. |
| **Selamat malam**   | Good evening / night       | After sunset.               |
| **Apa kabar?**      | How are you?               | Casual and friendly.        |
| **Baik.**           | I’m fine.                  | Standard reply.             |
| **Baik-baik saja.** | I’m doing just fine.       | Slightly more formal.       |

## Introducing Yourself
| English             | Indonesian                           | Notes                         |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| My name is...       | **Nama saya...**                     | Nama saya John.               |
| I am...             | **Saya...**                          | Saya pelajar. (I’m a student) |
| Nice to meet you    | **Senang bertemu denganmu**          | Polite and friendly           |
| Where are you from? | **Dari mana?** / **Asal dari mana?** | Casual & useful               |
| I’m from...         | **Saya dari...**                     | Saya dari Amerika.            |

## Saying Goodbye
| Indonesian          | Meaning                           | Notes                                 |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Sampai jumpa!**   | See you!                          | Literally: "Until meet"               |
| **Sampai nanti!**   | See you later!                    | "Until later"                         |
| **Selamat tinggal** | Goodbye (when *you* are staying)  | Used when the other person is leaving |
| **Selamat jalan**   | Goodbye (when *they* are staying) | Used when *you* are leaving           |

# Practice Sentences
Say these out loud. Practice makes fluency!

- Halo, nama saya [your name].
    - **Hello, my name is [your name]**
    - Literally: _**Hello, Name I/Me [your name]**_
- Selamat pagi! Apa kabar?
    - **Good Morning! How Are You?**
    - Literally: _**Merry morning! What News ?**_
- Saya dari Jepang.
    - **I am from Japan**
    - Literally: _**I/me from Japan**_
- Senang bertemu denganmu!
    - **Nice to meet you**
    - Literally: _**Happy meeting with you**_
- Sampai jumpa besok!
    - **See you tomorrow**
    - Literally: _**Until meet tomorrow**_
