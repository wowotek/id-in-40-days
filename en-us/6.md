# Day 6 – Telling Time ⏰

Time is essential in everyday communication — from scheduling to asking when something happens.

In Indonesian, telling time is **straightforward**, and uses some unique expressions that you'll find fun and useful!

## 🕒 Basic Time Structure

To say the time, use:

> **Jam + [number]**

“Jam” means **hour** or **o'clock**.

| Time     | Indonesian      | Meaning             |
|----------|------------------|---------------------|
| 1:00     | jam satu         | one o'clock         |
| 2:00     | jam dua          | two o'clock         |
| 3:00     | jam tiga         | three o'clock       |
| 10:00    | jam sepuluh      | ten o'clock         |

---

## 🕰️ Minutes & Past the Hour

Use:

> **Jam [hour] lewat [minutes]**  
> *(literally: hour past minutes)*

| Time   | Indonesian               | English             |
|--------|--------------------------|---------------------|
| 3:05   | jam tiga lewat lima      | 5 past 3            |
| 4:10   | jam empat lewat sepuluh  | 10 past 4           |
| 5:15   | jam lima lewat lima belas| 15 past 5           |

## 🕤 Half Past / Quarter To

| Time     | Indonesian                      | Meaning                  |
|----------|----------------------------------|--------------------------|
| 4:30     | **jam setengah lima**            | half *to* 5 (i.e., 4:30) |
| 6:45     | **jam tujuh kurang lima belas**  | 15 minutes *to* 7        |
| 2:50     | **jam tiga kurang sepuluh**      | 10 minutes *to* 3        |

> ⚠️ Indonesian uses “**setengah** [next hour]” for half-past!

## 🗓️ Asking and Saying Time

| English                    | Indonesian                  |
|----------------------------|-----------------------------|
| What time is it?           | **Jam berapa?**             |
| It's 9 o'clock.            | **Jam sembilan.**           |
| It's half past four.       | **Jam setengah lima.**      |
| It's 15 minutes to 8.      | **Jam delapan kurang lima belas.** |
| What time do you sleep?    | **Kamu tidur jam berapa?**  |
| I sleep at 11.             | **Saya tidur jam sebelas.** |

## 🕙 Telling Time of Day

You can also add **context of the day**:

| Phrase           | Meaning             |
|------------------|---------------------|
| pagi             | morning (4–10 AM)   |
| siang            | midday (11–2 PM)    |
| sore             | afternoon (3–6 PM)  |
| malam            | night (7–12 PM)     |

> Ex: **Jam tujuh pagi** = 7 AM  
>     **Jam delapan malam** = 8 PM
