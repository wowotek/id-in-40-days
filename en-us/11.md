# Day 11 – Asking for Directions 🧭

Today, you’ll learn how to **ask for and understand directions** using simple Indonesian — no prefixes, no suffixes.

This is super useful when traveling, finding places, or asking locals for help.

## 🧭 Key Question Phrases

| English                | Indonesian (Simplified)       |
|------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Where is [place]?      | [Tempat] **di mana**?         |
| How to go to [place]?  | **Bagaimana ke** [tempat]?    |
| Where are you going?   | **Kamu ke mana**?             |
| Is it far?             | **Jauh tidak?**               |
| Left or right?         | **Kiri atau kanan?**          |

## 🏙️ Important Direction Words

| Indonesian | English       |

| kiri       | left          |

| belok      | turn          |
| dekat      | near          |
| jauh       | far           |
| lampu merah| traffic light |

## 🏃‍♂️ Common Phrases

| English                        | Indonesian (Simplified)            |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Go straight                   | **Lurus** saja                     |
| Turn left                     | **Belok kiri**                     |
| Turn right                    | **Belok kanan**                    |
| It's near                     | **Dekat** saja                     |
| It's far                      | **Jauh sekali**                    |
| Go to the market              | **Ke pasar**                       |
| Where is the station?         | **Stasiun di mana?**              |
| How to go to the mosque?      | **Bagaimana ke masjid?**          |

> 💡 You can say **saja** to mean "just" or "only", and **sekali** to mean "very".

## 🗺️ Sample Dialog

**A**: Permisi, pasar di mana?  
**B**: Lurus saja, lalu belok kanan.  
**A**: Dekat?  
**B**: Ya, dekat.

> A: Excuse me, where is the market?  
> B: Go straight, then turn right.  
> A: Is it near?  
> B: Yes, near.
