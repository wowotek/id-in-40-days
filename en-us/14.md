# 🍽️ Day 14 — Food & Ordering

Today you'll learn how to order food, understand menus, and eat like a local in Indonesia. Whether you're at a street stall or a restaurant, these phrases will help you enjoy the culinary adventure!

---

## 📝 Ordering Food & Drinks

| Indonesian                        | English                        |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Saya mau pesan...                 | I want to order...             |
| Ada menu?                         | Do you have a menu?            |
| Apa rekomendasinya?               | What do you recommend?         |
| Tanpa pedas, ya.                  | No spicy, please.              |
| Tolong tambah sambal.             | Please add chili sauce.        |
| Satu porsi nasi goreng, ya.       | One portion of fried rice, please. |
| Minumnya apa?                     | What drinks do you have?       |
| Air putih saja.                   | Just water, please.            |
| Sudah cukup, terima kasih.        | That's enough, thank you.      |

---

## 🍴 Example Dialogue

**At a Warung (local eatery):**

> **Anda:** Selamat siang, ada menu?
> 
> **Penjual:** Ada, mau makan apa?
> 
> **Anda:** Saya mau pesan ayam goreng dan sayur, pedas sedikit.
> 
> **Penjual:** Minumnya?
> 
> **Anda:** Teh manis dingin, satu.
> 
> **Penjual:** Baik, tunggu sebentar ya.
