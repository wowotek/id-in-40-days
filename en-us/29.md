# Day 29 — Health and Going to the Doctor

Today you'll learn how to talk about health, symptoms, and visiting the doctor in Indonesian. This is important for your well-being!

---

## 🏥 Health Vocabulary

| Indonesian         | English           |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| sakit              | sick/ill          |
| demam              | fever             |
| batuk              | cough             |
| pilek              | cold (illness)    |
| pusing             | dizzy/headache    |
| perut              | stomach           |
| dokter             | doctor            |
| obat               | medicine          |
| klinik             | clinic            |
| rumah sakit        | hospital          |
| resep              | prescription      |

---

## 🗣️ Talking About Health

| Indonesian                              | English                          |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Saya sakit kepala.                      | I have a headache.               |
| Dia demam sejak kemarin.                | He/She has had a fever since yesterday. |
| Saya mau ke dokter.                     | I want to go to the doctor.      |
| Apakah ada apotek dekat sini?           | Is there a pharmacy nearby?      |
| Sudah minum obat?                       | Have you taken medicine?         |

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**At the clinic:**

> **Dokter:** Apa keluhan Anda?
> 
> **Pasien:** Saya batuk dan pusing.
> 
> **Dokter:** Sudah berapa lama?
> 
> **Pasien:** Dua hari, Dok.

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. I have a fever.
2. She wants to go to the hospital.
3. Do you have a headache?
4. Where is the clinic?
5. Have you taken medicine?

✅ _Answer Key:_

1. Saya demam.
2. Dia mau ke rumah sakit.
3. Kamu sakit kepala?
4. Di mana klinik?
5. Sudah minum obat?
