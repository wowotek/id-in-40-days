# 📝 Day 18 — Descriptions & Adjectives

Today you'll learn how to describe people, objects, and places in Indonesian. This will help you add detail and color to your conversations!

---

---

## 🗣️ Using Adjectives

| Indonesian                        | English                        |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Rumah itu besar.                  | That house is big.             |
| Anjingnya lucu sekali.            | The dog is very cute.          |
| Dia orang yang pintar.            | He/She is a smart person.      |
| Mobil saya baru.                  | My car is new.                 |
| Jalan ini panjang.                | This road is long.             |
| Kamar itu bersih.                 | That room is clean.            |

---

## 🏠 Example Dialogue

**Describing a friend:**

> **Anda:** Teman kamu seperti apa?
> 
> **Teman:** Dia tinggi dan sangat pintar.
> 
> **Anda:** Orangnya lucu juga?
> 
> **Teman:** Iya, dia sangat lucu!

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. That cat is very cute.
2. My house is small but clean.
3. He is strong and smart.
4. This road is long.
5. The car is new.

✅ _Answer Key:_

1. Kucing itu lucu sekali.
2. Rumah saya kecil tapi bersih.
3. Dia kuat dan pintar.
4. Jalan ini panjang.
5. Mobilnya baru.
