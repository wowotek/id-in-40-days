# 🛍️ Day 13 — Shopping & Prices

Today you’ll learn how to shop, ask for prices, and negotiate in Indonesian — essential for local markets and daily life!

---

---

## 🧾 Asking About Prices

| Indonesian                        | English                        |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Berapa harganya?                  | How much is it?               |
| Harganya berapa?                  | What’s the price?             |
| Bisa kurang?                      | Can you lower the price?      |
| Ada diskon?                       | Is there a discount?          |
| Mahal sekali!                     | That's very expensive!        |
| Murah!                            | Cheap!                        |
| Saya hanya lihat-lihat.           | I’m just looking.             |
| Saya mau beli ini.                | I want to buy this.           |

---

## 💰 Numbers Reminder

Make sure you remember numbers from [Day 5](5.md) — you’ll need them for prices!

Example:
- **Seratus ribu rupiah** = 100,000 IDR
- **Lima puluh ribu** = 50,000
- **Sepuluh ribu** = 10,000

---

## 🛒 Useful Expressions in Context

| Situation                    | What to Say in Indonesian                        |
|-----------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Asking for price            | "Berapa harganya, Bu?" *(How much, ma’am?)*     |
| Negotiating politely        | "Bisa kurang sedikit?" *(Can you lower a bit?)* |
| Confirming total            | "Semua berapa?" *(How much for everything?)*    |
| Asking for change           | "Kembaliannya berapa?" *(How much is the change?)* |

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. How much is this bag?  
2. That’s too expensive.  
3. Do you have a cheaper one?  
4. I want to buy two.  
5. I’m just looking, thanks.

1. Tas ini berapa harganya?  
2. Itu terlalu mahal.  
3. Ada yang lebih murah?  
4. Saya mau beli dua.
