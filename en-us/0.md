# Day 0 – Introduction
## Why This Book Exists
This book is part of my personal mission to promote Bahasa Indonesia as the next international language, to one day “replace English” as the global medium of communication.

Bahasa Indonesia is simple, elegant, and logical. It deserves global recognition, and through this book, I hope to make it accessible to everyone, everywhere.

By learning it, you're not just picking up a new language, you're joining a movement. I want learners like you to discover how practical and elegant this language is, and how learning it can open up real cultural, personal, and professional connections across Southeast Asia and beyond.

## How to Use This Book
To get the most out of your 40-day journey:

- Commit 40 minutes to 1 hour each day. Learning a language isn’t about cramming, it’s about consistency.
- Complete one chapter per day, each one is structured to be practical and digestible.
- Speak out loud, Indonesian is a phonetic language, and speaking helps lock in muscle memory.
- Follow the daily flow, vocab, patterns, pronunciation, and usage.

If you do this, by Day 40, you'll not just know “some phrases”, you'll be able to speak Indonesian confidently like a native in everyday situations.
