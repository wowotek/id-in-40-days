# 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Day 16 — Talking About Family

Today you'll learn how to talk about your family and ask about others' families in Indonesian. Great for making personal connections!

---

---

## 🗣️ Talking About Family

| Indonesian                        | English                        |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Saya punya dua adik.              | I have two younger siblings.   |
| Keluarga saya besar.              | My family is big.              |
| Ini ayah dan ibu saya.            | This is my father and mother.  |
| Apakah kamu punya saudara?        | Do you have siblings?          |
| Anak saya masih kecil.            | My child is still young.       |
| Dia kakak perempuan saya.         | She is my older sister.        |

---

## 🏡 Example Dialogue

**Meeting a friend:**

> **Anda:** Kamu punya berapa saudara?
> 
> **Teman:** Aku punya satu kakak dan satu adik.
> 
> **Anda:** Wah, keluarga kamu besar ya!
> 
> **Teman:** Lumayan! Keluargamu?
> 
> **Anda:** Saya anak tunggal.

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. My family is small.
2. I have one brother and one sister.
3. This is my grandmother.
4. Do you have children?
5. My father's name is Budi.

✅ _Answer Key:_

1. Keluarga saya kecil.
2. Saya punya satu kakak laki-laki dan satu adik perempuan.
3. Ini nenek saya.
4. Apakah kamu punya anak?
5. Nama ayah saya Budi.
