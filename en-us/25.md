# Day 25 — Shopping and Bargaining

Today you'll learn how to shop and negotiate prices in Indonesian. These skills are essential for markets and daily life!

---

## 🛒 Shopping Vocabulary

| Indonesian         | English           |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| berapa             | how much          |
| harga              | price             |
| murah              | cheap             |
| mahal              | expensive         |
| diskon             | discount          |
| tawar              | to bargain        |
| beli               | to buy            |
| jual               | to sell           |
| toko               | shop/store        |
| pasar              | market            |

---

## 🗣️ Shopping and Bargaining

| Indonesian                              | English                          |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Berapa harganya?                        | How much is it?                  |
| Bisa kurang?                            | Can it be cheaper?               |
| Ada diskon?                             | Is there a discount?             |
| Ini terlalu mahal.                      | This is too expensive.           |
| Saya mau beli dua.                      | I want to buy two.               |
| Di mana pasar terdekat?                 | Where is the nearest market?     |

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**At the market:**

> **Anda:** Berapa harga baju ini?
> 
> **Penjual:** Seratus ribu rupiah.
> 
> **Anda:** Bisa kurang?
> 
> **Penjual:** Bisa, delapan puluh ribu saja.

---
