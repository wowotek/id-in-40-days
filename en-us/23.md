# Day 23 — Talking About Daily Routines

Today you'll learn how to describe your everyday activities in Indonesian. This is useful for sharing your schedule and habits!

---

## 🕒 Daily Routine Vocabulary

| Indonesian         | English           |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| bangun             | wake up           |
| mandi              | shower/bathe      |
| sarapan            | breakfast         |
| berangkat          | depart/leave      |
| bekerja            | work              |
| belajar            | study             |
| makan siang        | lunch             |
| pulang             | go home           |
| tidur              | sleep             |
| setiap hari        | every day         |
| biasanya           | usually           |

---

## 🗣️ Talking About Routines

| Indonesian                              | English                          |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Saya bangun jam enam pagi.              | I wake up at six in the morning. |
| Setelah mandi, saya sarapan.            | After showering, I have breakfast.|
| Dia berangkat kerja jam tujuh.          | He/She leaves for work at seven. |
| Kami makan siang bersama.               | We have lunch together.          |
| Biasanya saya tidur jam sepuluh malam.  | I usually sleep at ten at night. |

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**Describing a daily routine:**

> **Anda:** Apa rutinitas harianmu?
> 
> **Teman:** Saya bangun jam enam, lalu mandi dan sarapan.
> 
> **Anda:** Setelah itu?
> 
> **Teman:** Saya berangkat kerja jam tujuh dan pulang sore.

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. I wake up at seven.
2. She goes to work every day.
3. We have lunch at noon.
4. He usually sleeps at nine.
5. After studying, I go home.

✅ _Answer Key:_

1. Saya bangun jam tujuh.
2. Dia bekerja setiap hari.
3. Kami makan siang jam dua belas.
4. Dia biasanya tidur jam sembilan.
5. Setelah belajar, saya pulang.
