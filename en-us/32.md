# Day 32 — Family and Relationships

Today you'll learn how to talk about your family, friends, and relationships in Indonesian. This is important for personal conversations!

---

## 👪 Family & Relationship Vocabulary

| Indonesian         | English           |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| keluarga           | family            |
| ayah               | father            |
| ibu                | mother            |
| anak               | child             |
| saudara            | sibling/relative  |
| kakak              | older sibling     |
| adik               | younger sibling   |
| teman              | friend            |
| pasangan           | partner/spouse    |
| menikah            | married           |
| pacar              | boyfriend/girlfriend |

---

## 🗣️ Talking About Family and Relationships

| Indonesian                              | English                          |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Saya punya dua saudara.                 | I have two siblings.             |
| Ayah saya bekerja di Jakarta.           | My father works in Jakarta.      |
| Ibu saya suka memasak.                  | My mother likes to cook.         |
| Dia sudah menikah.                      | He/She is married.               |
| Kami berteman sejak kecil.              | We have been friends since childhood. |

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**Talking about family:**

> **Anda:** Berapa saudara kamu?
> 
> **Teman:** Saya punya satu kakak dan satu adik.
> 
> **Anda:** Apakah kamu sudah menikah?
> 
> **Teman:** Belum, saya masih lajang.

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. I have three siblings.
2. She is married.
3. My mother is a teacher.
4. We are friends.
5. He is my older brother.

✅ _Answer Key:_

1. Saya punya tiga saudara.
2. Dia sudah menikah.
3. Ibu saya guru.
4. Kami berteman.
5. Dia kakak laki-laki saya.
