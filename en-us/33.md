# Day 33 — Celebrations and Holidays

Today you'll learn how to talk about special occasions, holidays, and celebrations in Indonesian. This is great for sharing joy and traditions!

---

## 🎉 Celebrations & Holidays Vocabulary

| Indonesian         | English           |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| ulang tahun        | birthday          |
| perayaan           | celebration       |
| hari libur         | holiday           |
| Idul Fitri         | Eid al-Fitr       |
| Natal              | Christmas         |
| Tahun Baru         | New Year          |
| pesta              | party             |
| undangan           | invitation        |
| kue                | cake              |
| hadiah             | gift/present      |
| merayakan          | to celebrate      |

---

## 🗣️ Talking About Celebrations

| Indonesian                              | English                          |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Saya merayakan ulang tahun kemarin.     | I celebrated my birthday yesterday. |
| Kami mengadakan pesta kecil.            | We had a small party.             |
| Selamat Idul Fitri!                     | Happy Eid al-Fitr!                |
| Apa rencanamu untuk Tahun Baru?         | What are your plans for New Year? |
| Saya dapat hadiah dari teman.           | I got a present from a friend.    |

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**Talking about holidays:**

> **Anda:** Kapan ulang tahunmu?
> 
> **Teman:** Bulan depan. Saya akan merayakan dengan keluarga.
> 
> **Anda:** Selamat ya! Semoga menyenangkan.

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. Happy birthday!
2. We celebrate Christmas.
3. I got a gift.
4. What is your favorite holiday?
5. I will have a party tomorrow.

✅ _Answer Key:_

1. Selamat ulang tahun!
2. Kami merayakan Natal.
3. Saya dapat hadiah.
4. Hari libur favoritmu apa?
5. Saya akan pesta besok.
