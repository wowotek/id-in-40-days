# Day 24 — Describing People and Things

Today you'll learn how to describe people and objects using adjectives and comparisons in Indonesian. This will help you express details and opinions!

---

## 👤 Descriptive Vocabulary

| Indonesian         | English           |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| tinggi             | tall              |
| pendek             | short             |
| besar              | big               |
| kecil              | small             |
| cantik             | beautiful         |
| tampan             | handsome          |
| pintar             | smart             |
| kuat               | strong            |
| cepat              | fast              |
| lambat             | slow              |

| muda               | young             |

---

## 🗣️ Describing and Comparing

| Indonesian                              | English                          |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Dia tinggi dan pintar.                  | He/She is tall and smart.        |
| Rumah itu besar.                        | That house is big.               |
| Mobil ini lebih cepat dari itu.         | This car is faster than that one.|
| Anjing saya kecil tapi kuat.            | My dog is small but strong.      |
| Dia lebih tua dari saya.                | He/She is older than me.         |

---

## 🕵️ Example Dialogue

**Describing a friend:**

> **Anda:** Temanmu seperti apa?
> 
> **Teman:** Dia cantik dan sangat pintar.
> 
> **Anda:** Siapa yang lebih tinggi, kamu atau dia?
> 
> **Teman:** Dia lebih tinggi dari saya.

---

## 🧠 Practice

Translate into Indonesian:

1. He is strong and smart.
2. This house is small.
3. She is younger than me.
4. My car is faster.
5. That dog is old.

✅ _Answer Key:_

1. Dia kuat dan pintar.
2. Rumah ini kecil.
3. Dia lebih muda dari saya.
4. Mobil saya lebih cepat.
5. Anjing itu tua.
