# Day 10 – Jobs & Hobbies 👩‍💼🎸

Today, learn how to talk about your **job** and your **hobby** — in simple Indonesian with no complicated word changes!

In this method, we use only **root words**. No prefixes. No suffixes. Just the core!

---

## 💼 Talking About Jobs

Say:

> **Saya [job]**  
> (*I am [job]*)

You don’t need "adalah" unless you want to sound formal. Keep it simple!

| Job (Indonesian) | English         |
|------------------|------------------|
| guru             | teacher          |
| dokter           | doctor           |
| siswa            | student          |
| karyawan         | employee         |
| usaha            | entrepreneur (lit. "business") |
| polisi           | police officer   |
| perawat          | nurse            |
| supir            | driver           |
| petani           | farmer           |
| seni             | artist (lit. art person)        |

> 🗣 Example:  
> Saya **guru**. → I’m a teacher.  
> Dia **dokter**. → He/She is a doctor.

---

## 🎨 Talking About Hobbies

Say:

> **Saya suka [activity / thing]**  
> (*I like...*)

| Hobby (Indonesian) | English         |
|---------------------|------------------|
| baca                | reading          |
| tulis               | writing          |
| masak               | cooking          |
| main musik          | play music       |
| nyanyi              | singing          |
| nonton film         | watch movies     |
| olahraga            | exercise         |
| main game           | play games       |
| lukis               | paint            |
| jalan               | travel / walk    |

> 🗣 Example:  
> Saya suka **masak**. → I like to cook.  
> Dia suka **main gitar**. → He/She likes playing guitar.

## 🔁 Combining Jobs & Hobbies

Say:

> **Saya [job] dan saya suka [activity]**

Examples:

- Saya **siswa**, saya suka **main game**.  
- Saya **usaha**, saya suka **jalan**.  
- Dia **seni**, dia suka **lukis**.

---

## 🚀 Challenge of the Day

Write or say:

1. I am a student and I like watching movies.  
2. He is a doctor and he likes to cook.  
3. We are artists and we like music.  
4. What is your job?  
5. What do you like?

Use only root words — keep it easy and natural!
