## Grammar Review

*   **Basic sentence structure:** Subject + Verb + Object
    *   Example: `Saya makan nasi.` (मैं चावल खाता हूँ।) - I eat rice.
*   **Using `punya` for possession:**
    *   Example: `Saya punya buku.` (मेरे पास एक किताब है।) - I have a book.
*   **Prepositions of place `di`, `ke`, `dari`:**
    *   Example: `Saya di rumah.` (मैं घर पर हूँ।) - I am at home.
    *   `Saya pergi ke pasar.` (मैं बाज़ार जाता हूँ।) - I go to the market.
    *   `Saya datang dari kantor.` (मैं कार्यालय से आया हूँ।) - I came from the office.
*   **Verb prefixes `me-` and `ber-`:**
    *   `membaca` (पढ़ना) from `baca`
    *   `berbicara` (बात करना) from `bicara`
*   **Comparatives and superlatives of adjectives:**
    *   `lebih... daripada...` (से अधिक...)
    *   `paling...` (सबसे अधिक...)

## Next Steps

These 40 days of learning are just the beginning of your Indonesian language journey. Here are some suggestions to continue improving:

1.  **Regular review:** Regularly review the learned vocabulary and grammar to ensure you retain them.
2.  **Conversational practice:** Look for opportunities to speak with native Indonesian speakers. Online language exchange partners or local communities can be helpful.
3.  **Media utilization:** Watch Indonesian movies, TV shows, listen to Indonesian music, and podcasts to improve your listening comprehension.
4.  **Reading:** Start reading simple Indonesian books, news articles, and blogs to enhance your reading comprehension and expand your vocabulary.
5.  **Set new goals:** Set new learning goals, such as improving your conversation skills on specific topics or mastering more complex grammar.
6.  **Cultural exploration:** Learn more about Indonesia's culture, history, and customs to deepen your understanding of the language.
