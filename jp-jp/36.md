## 2. Expressing Feelings

Use these structures to talk about how you or others are feeling.

*   **Using "I feel..." or "I am...":**
    *   `I am happy.` (Saya senang.)
    *   `He is tired.` (Dia lelah.)

*   **Asking about feelings:**
    *   `How are you feeling?` (Bagaimana perasaanmu?)
    *   `Are you hungry?` (Apakah kamu lapar?)

## 3. Common Expressions about Emotions

*   `I'm fine, thank you.` (Saya baik-baik saja, terima kasih.)
*   `Don't worry.` (Jangan khawatir.)
*   `Cheer up!` (Semangat! / Bersoraklah!)
*   `Take care.` (Hati-hati.)

## 4. Useful Phrases

*   `I am happy today.` (Saya senang hari ini.)
*   `Are you sad?` (Apakah kamu sedih?)
*   `I'm very tired.` (Saya sangat lelah.)
*   `She is excited.` (Dia bersemangat.)

## 5. Mini-Dialogue

**A:** `How are you feeling today?` (Bagaimana perasaanmu hari ini?)
**B:** `I'm a little tired, but also happy.` (Saya sedikit lelah, tapi juga senang.)
**A:** `Why are you happy?` (Mengapa kamu senang?)
**B:** `Because I finished my work early.` (Karena saya menyelesaikan pekerjaan saya lebih awal.)
