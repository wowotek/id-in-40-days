## 2. Discussing Holiday Traditions

Use these structures to talk about holiday activities and traditions.

*   **Talking about activities:**
    *   `visiting family` (mengunjungi keluarga)
    *   `eating special food` (makan makanan khusus)
    *   `exchanging gifts` (bertukar hadiah)

    *Examples: *
    *   `We visit family during Eid al-Fitr.` (Kami mengunjungi keluarga saat Idul Fitri.)
    *   `They eat special cake for Christmas.` (Mereka makan kue khusus untuk Natal.)

*   **Expressing well wishes:**
    *   `Happy New Year!` (Selamat Tahun Baru!)
    *   `Merry Christmas!` (Selamat Natal!)
    *   `Happy Eid!` (Selamat Idul Fitri!)

## 3. Common Expressions about Celebrations

*   `Happy birthday!` (Selamat ulang tahun!)
*   `Enjoy your holiday!` (Selamat berlibur!)
*   `Have a good time!` (Selamat bersenang-senang!)

## 4. Useful Phrases

*   `What are your plans for the holiday?` (Apa rencanamu untuk liburan?)
*   `We celebrate Eid al-Fitr every year.` (Kami merayakan Idul Fitri setiap tahun.)
*   `I like to give gifts.` (Saya suka memberi hadiah.)
*   `The party was fun.` (Pestanya menyenangkan.)

## 5. Mini-Dialogue

**A:** `What are your plans for New Year\'s Day?` (Apa rencanamu untuk Tahun Baru?)
**B:** `I will visit my family and we will have a party.` (Saya akan mengunjungi keluarga saya dan kami akan mengadakan pesta.)
**A:** `That sounds fun! Will you exchange gifts?` (Kedengarannya menyenangkan! Akankah kalian bertukar hadiah?)
**B:** `Yes, we always do. And we will eat special cake.` (Ya, kami selalu begitu. Dan kami akan makan kue khusus.)
