## 2. Discussing Nature and Environment

Use these structures to talk about nature and environmental concerns.

*   **Talking about favorite places:**
    *   `I like to go to the beach.` (Saya suka pergi ke pantai.)
    *   `He loves the forest.` (Dia suka hutan.)

*   **Expressing concern:**
    *   `We need to protect the environment.` (Kita perlu melindungi lingkungan.)
    *   `Pollution is a big problem.` (Polusi adalah masalah besar.)

## 3. Common Expressions about Nature

*   `Beautiful view!` (Pemandangan indah!)
*   `Fresh air!` (Udara segar!)
*   `Clean water.` (Air bersih.)
*   `Protect our planet.` (Lindungi planet kita.)

## 4. Useful Phrases

*   `I love the mountains.` (Saya suka gunung.)
*   `The beach is very clean.` (Pantainya sangat bersih.)
*   `It's raining today.` (Hari ini hujan.)
*   `We need to protect our planet.` (Kita perlu melindungi planet kita.)

## 5. Mini-Dialogue

**A:** `Have you ever visited Mount Bromo?` (Apakah kamu pernah mengunjungi Gunung Bromo?)
**B:** `Yes, it has a beautiful view! I love nature.` (Ya, pemandangannya indah sekali! Saya suka alam.)
**A:** `Me too. We should always keep our environment clean.` (Saya juga. Kita harus selalu menjaga lingkungan kita tetap bersih.)
**B:** `Exactly. Protecting nature is very important.` (Tepat sekali. Melindungi alam sangat penting.)
